engine-sound

Nothing here is a recording. Cylinder pressure, valve timing and exhaust gas dynamics are simulated at 10 kHz on the audio thread. Drop in an engine-sim .mr file or a JSON engine definition to hear a different one — there are examples beside this page.

Pick a gear and hold Space for full throttle; N is neutral, and the number keys change gear. The starter disengages by itself once the engine catches, and gives up after a few seconds if it doesn't. Enough load will stall the engine — back the load off before cranking, or it just spins at starter speed like the real thing.
